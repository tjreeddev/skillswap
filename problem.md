# Problem

Freelancers and small business owners often need services they can't easily
afford, like a logo, tax help, a website fix, or tutoring. Many of them have a
skill someone else needs, but there's no simple way to find a person who wants
what they offer and offers what they need. Finding a trade partner today means
asking around or posting in group chats and hoping someone answers.

# Initial requirements

- R1. A user can create a profile with a name, or choose an existing profile.
- R2. A user can add services they offer and services they want to their profile.
- R3. The app shows a list of matches: other users who offer something this user
  wants and want something this user offers.
- R4. Profiles and their services are saved to a file and are still there after
  the app is restarted.
- R5. The app runs on the user's own computer with one command and is used in a
  browser.
